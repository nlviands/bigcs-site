Why a non-CDL reefer fleet

Right-sized trucks, fewer bottlenecks

Our trucks sit just under the 26,001 lb line where a commercial driver's license kicks in. For a distributor, that changes what the truck can do.

  • 1
    Any qualified driver can run it. No CDL pipeline to staff around, so a route does not go dark when one licensed driver is out.
  • 2
    Fits the docks a 53-footer cannot. Downtown restaurants, school kitchens, and strip-center back doors were built for a box truck.
  • 3
    Sized to a route, not a warehouse. Up to roughly 1,600 cu ft and 8,000 lb of payload per truck, which is a full day of stops without running half-empty.
  • 4
    Two trucks, one owner. You deal with the person who drives it. Problems get solved on the phone, not in a ticket queue.
